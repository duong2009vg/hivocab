1. Application Overview & Purpose

HiVocab is an educational web application specifically designed to assist English language learners, high school students preparing for the National High School Graduation Exam (THPT Quốc Gia), and IELTS candidates.

The application provides curated vocabulary lists from Cambridge IELTS (10–21) and IELTS Actual Tests (Vol 1–9), bilingual reading comprehension tools (Curtain Mode), and an automated Spaced Repetition System (SRS SM-2) that schedules timely reviews to build lasting memory retention.

2. How HiVocab Accesses Google User Data (Scopes)

When you initiate authentication using "Continue with Google" (Google Sign-In), HiVocab accesses only basic, non-sensitive identity information through standard OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect scopes:

Scope Data Accessed Specific Need & Purpose
openid User Identifier Token Authenticates your session securely using standard OpenID Connect protocol.
.../auth/userinfo.email Email address Identifies your unique account, enables secure sign-in, and sends essential account security notifications.
.../auth/userinfo.profile Full name & profile picture Displays personalized greetings in your study dashboard and credits your name if you publish decks to the Community Library.
Explicit Limitation: HiVocab NEVER requests, accesses, or reads sensitive Google scopes such as Google Drive files, Gmail inboxes, Contacts, Google Calendar, Device Location, or Financial data.

3. How HiVocab Uses Google User Data

HiVocab uses information obtained from Google OAuth solely to provide and support core educational features:

  • Authenticating user identity and maintaining secure login sessions.
  • Creating your personal learning profile and synchronizing your custom vocabulary notebooks.
  • Tracking individual memory retention intervals calculated by the SuperMemo-2 (SM-2) algorithm.
  • Saving your Cambridge IELTS reading progress, bilingual comprehension notes, and practice exam scores.

HiVocab does NOT use Google user data for advertising, marketing profiling, or commercial targeting of any kind.

4. How HiVocab Stores and Protects Google User Data

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • Database Architecture: User records are stored on Supabase managed cloud infrastructure hosted in enterprise-grade data centers.
  • Encryption in Transit: All data transferred between your browser and our servers is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / SSL HTTPS).
  • Encryption at Rest: Stored databases, backups, and user records are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standard encryption.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Strict database policies enforce user-level isolation, guaranteeing that you can only access and modify your own study data.

5. How HiVocab Shares or Discloses Google User Data

Zero Selling & Zero Third-Party Sharing: HiVocab NEVER sells, rents, leases, trades, or commercializes your personal information or Google user data to any third party, advertising network, or data broker under any circumstances.

Google user data is shared only with our core cloud infrastructure provider (Supabase) strictly for hosting and executing the application services, under legally binding data confidentiality agreements.

AI Model Training Prohibition: HiVocab does NOT use Google user data to train, retrain, or fine-tune artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

HiVocab's use of information received from Google APIs strictly complies with Google's requirements:

"HiVocab's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements."

7. Data Retention and Permanent Deletion Procedures

You maintain complete authority and control over your personal data:

Retention Period:

We retain your profile information and learning records for as long as your account remains active. If an account is completely inactive for twenty-four (24) consecutive months, all associated personal records are automatically and permanently purged.

In-App Account Deletion:

You can delete your account and all associated vocabulary lists at any time by navigating to Settings (Cài đặt) > Delete My Account (Xóa tài khoản của tôi). All records will be erased immediately and irrevocably.

Email Deletion Request:

You may submit an account and data erasure request via email to support@hivocab.site or mitthoi60@gmail.com. We verify your ownership and complete permanent data deletion within seven (7) business days.

Revoking Google Permissions:

You can immediately revoke HiVocab's access to your Google Account at any time through your Google Account Security Permissions Manager.

8. Policy Updates and User Notification

We keep this Privacy Policy current and reflective of our actual practices. If we change how HiVocab accesses, uses, stores, or shares Google user data, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy page.
  • Display a prominent notification banner directly within the application interface upon your next login.
  • Notify registered users via their registered email address at least thirty (30) days before any material changes in data usage take effect.
