Privacy Policy

Application: HiVocab (https://hivocab.site) · Effective Date: September 15, 2026

1. Introduction & Application Purpose

HiVocab (https://hivocab.site) is an educational web application designed to help students, IELTS test-takers, and English language learners acquire and retain academic vocabulary through the Spaced Repetition System (SRS SM-2) and Active Reading (Curtain Mode).

We respect your privacy and are committed to transparently disclosing how we access, collect, use, store, and protect your information, particularly data obtained through Google OAuth (Google User Data).

2. Google User Data We Collect & Scopes Requested

When you sign in using "Continue with Google" (Google Sign-In), HiVocab requests only the following non-sensitive standard scopes:

  • openid: Used to securely verify your identity based on OpenID Connect protocol.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email (Email): Used as your unique account identifier to create and manage your study account.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile (Profile): Used to retrieve your name and profile picture to display personalized greetings in the app and show author credit on shared vocabulary decks.
Scope Limitation: HiVocab does NOT request, access, or read any sensitive Google scopes, such as Google Drive files, Gmail messages, Contacts, Google Calendar, or Location data.

3. How We Access and Use Your Data

Google user data is accessed only when you authenticate and is used strictly for core educational features:

  • Authenticating user login sessions.
  • Creating and maintaining your personal vocabulary notebook.
  • Tracking and scheduling Spaced Repetition (SM-2) memory reviews.
  • Saving your IELTS reading practice progress and test history.

4. Data Storage and Security

User account information is stored securely on Supabase cloud database infrastructure:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS (HTTPS) and at rest using AES-256 standards.
  • Access Control: Database Row-Level Security (RLS) is enforced, ensuring each user can only view and edit their own study data.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Zero Selling Policy: HiVocab NEVER sells, rents, leases, or trades your personal information or Google user data to any third party, advertising company, or data broker.

Furthermore, HiVocab does NOT use Google user data to train, retrain, or fine-tune artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

HiVocab's use of Google user data adheres strictly to Google's requirements:

"HiVocab's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements."

7. Data Retention & Deletion Procedures

Users retain full ownership and control of their data:

  • Retention: Personal data is kept for as long as your account remains active. If an account is inactive for 24 months, it is scheduled for automated permanent deletion.
  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated vocabulary data at any time under Settings > Delete My Account.
  • Email Deletion Request: You can email us at support@hivocab.site or mitthoi60@gmail.com to request account deletion. We process all deletion requests within 7 business days.
  • Revoke Google Permissions: You can revoke HiVocab's access to your Google account at any time via Google Account Security Settings.
